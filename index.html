Help & FAQ

How do I install an APK?
  1. Tap Download APK on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, open Lumenous, and choose it as your home app.
Which phones does it work on?

Any phone with Android 13 or newer and a 64-bit processor. It's designed first for square 1:1 screens with keyboards (Zinwa Q25, Unihertz Titan 2) — try it free on yours and see.

I bought a key. Where do I put it?

Your key is in your Gumroad receipt email and your Gumroad Library. In Lumenous, open Settings → Lumenous License, paste it and tap Activate. You need internet for that one moment; after that it works offline.

What happens when the trial ends?

Lumenous still works as your home screen and opens all your apps. Glass effects, themes and imported fonts switch off, and a small reminder appears now and then. Your settings are kept, so everything comes back the moment you unlock.

How do I update?

Lumenous tells you in Settings when a new version is out. Download it from this page and install it over the old one. Your settings and license stay.

Can I use one key on more than one phone?

Yes, a key works on a few of your own devices. If you hit a limit, email me and I'll sort it out.

How do I go back to my old home screen?

Open Settings → Lumenous License → Choose a different home app, or Android's Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app.
